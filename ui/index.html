How a request moves through the system

Every service on Land Stack follows the same three stages, so once you've done one, you know how to do the rest.

Step 1

Search your parcel

Enter your survey number, ULPIN, or village and plot details. We match it to the official cadastral map.

Step 2

Check what's on record

Review ownership, boundaries, dues and any pending requests linked to that parcel, pulled live from each department.

Step 3

Act or request a service

Pay, apply, or download a document. You'll get an application number and can track it from your dashboard.

Government-verified data

Every record comes directly from the land, registration and revenue departments — nothing is entered by us.

Your data stays protected

Ownership details are only shown after identity verification, and every access is recorded in an audit trail.

Works in your language

Search, forms and certificates are available in the regional languages your local office already uses.

Still not sure where to start?

I don't know my survey number or ULPIN

Search by your name and village instead — pick the "Owner name" tab above. If more than one record matches, we'll show you the addresses so you can pick the right one.

The ownership details shown don't match what I know

Use "Request a correction" on the record page. It goes straight to your local revenue officer, and you can track its progress from your dashboard.

Can someone else misuse my land information?

Basic parcel and map details are public, as they are at your local office. Ownership, tax and application details need OTP verification before they're shown.

What if my village isn't on Land Stack yet

Land Stack is being introduced state by state. If your village isn't listed yet, you can still visit your local office as before — nothing changes on your end.
